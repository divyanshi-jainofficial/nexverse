👾 ABOUT US

WHO IS NEXVERSE?

The official tech society of IITM Janakpuri — a community of student builders, coders, designers & dreamers.

01. OUR STORY

BORN FROM CURIOSITY

NexVerse-IITM was founded by a group of students at IITM Janakpuri who believed college life should be more than lectures and exams. It should be a place where ideas ship, code compiles, and friendships are built at 3 AM over pizza and pull requests.

From humble beginnings organizing small workshops in classrooms, we've grown into a national-level tech community — hosting India's most ambitious student-led AI hackathons, mentorship sessions with industry leaders, and creative tech events that spark real change.

We believe technology is a language everyone should speak, and we're here to teach the accent.
